Key Stories

LADWP remains a rebuild bottleneck

The Palisadian-Post lays out three utility friction points: temporary power before construction, LADWP’s policy of waiting until a home is about 90% framed before determining the permanent meter location, and paper-driven processing before permanent service starts.

Practical takeaway: if you are rebuilding, utility sequencing belongs on the critical path, not as a late-stage checklist item.

PCH truck crash prompts rescue and building checks

A flatbed truck hit a commercial building at 14830 W. Pacific Coast Highway on Thursday. FOX 11 reported a 90-plus-minute rescue before the driver was freed and taken to a hospital; LAFD said Building and Safety, Transportation and LAPD were notified.

No one was reported inside the business at the time, according to CBS Los Angeles.

Library rebuild debate shifts toward a civic hub

The Palisadian-Post published resident responses supporting a broader vision for the Palisades Library as a community and cultural center. The useful signal is not a final decision; it is that the rebuild conversation is drawing visible local support for a larger civic role.

Business Block preservation becomes a resident identity issue

A letter in the Palisadian-Post argues that the burned Business Block should not be treated only as an eyesore or redevelopment asset. It frames preservation as part of the community’s postfire identity and history.

Resident takeaway: this is opinion, not an official action, but it shows where local sentiment may pressure rebuild decisions.
