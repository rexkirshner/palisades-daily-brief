Key Stories

Candidate meet-and-greet gives residents direct access to runoff candidates

Pali Recovery Coalition lists a nonpartisan meet-and-greet today, July 26, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Pali Recovery HQ, 15233 La Cruz Dr. The practical value is access: residents can ask candidates directly about recovery, rebuilding, and city-service priorities.

Palisades Library keeps youth programs going from the green bungalow

The Palisadian-Post reports that Friends of the Palisades Library hosted creative writing workshops for students ages 6 to 18, and more children’s programs are scheduled in the library’s green bungalow. Story-Craft Time is listed for July 30 and August 6 at 3:30 p.m.; Baby and Toddler Storytime returns August 5 at 10:30 a.m.

Utility delays remain a move-back friction point for rebuilt homes

A Yahoo/The Cool Down report says some Palisades fire survivors have returned to rebuilt homes with certificates of occupancy but only limited temporary power. For residents rebuilding, the takeaway is to track LADWP service status as a separate move-in risk, not just permitting or construction completion.

Decisions Affecting Palisades

Metro board recommends adding a voting rider representative

Palisades News reports that the Metro Board unanimously recommended governance changes, including a voting rider representative and appointment changes tied to Los Angeles County’s Measure G restructuring. This is not Palisades-specific service news, but it affects the regional transit agency that shapes Westside mobility and major infrastructure decisions.

Fire/Rebuild Watch

Hillside rebuilds need erosion planning before rain becomes the test

A geotechnical guidance article aimed at Palisades and Eaton fire-affected hillside owners warns that burned slopes can shed water, mud, and rock differently after vegetation loss and soil changes. Practical takeaway: for hillside properties, retaining walls, drainage, erosion blankets, and inspections are core rebuild planning, not cosmetic extras.

Permit-fee waiver guidance highlights eligibility and resale limits

A rebuild guidance post summarizes Los Angeles’ fire-rebuild permit-fee waivers, including the 110% original-floor-area threshold, ownership-date eligibility, and a clawback if most waived-fee properties are sold before final certificate of occupancy. Treat this as useful planning background and confirm details with the city before relying on it.

Factory-built construction is being pitched as a faster return-home path

The Home Gallery says it installed the first factory-built home in Pacific Palisades after the January 2025 fires, describing a 1,700-square-foot main residence plus 600-square-foot ADU. The resident takeaway is that alternative construction methods are becoming part of the local rebuild conversation, especially for timing-sensitive families.

Insurance-claim expert documentation remains background

A property-insurance law blog uses Palisades and Altadena wildfire claims to explain why industrial hygienists, contractors, contents experts, and financial experts can matter in proving covered losses. Useful if you are in a disputed claim, but it is legal-process background rather than a new local deadline.
