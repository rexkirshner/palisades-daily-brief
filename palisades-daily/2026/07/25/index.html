Key Stories

PCH and Topanga work zones may slow local travel next week

Crews are scheduled to continue Palisades Fire recovery work along PCH and Topanga Canyon Boulevard from July 27 through Aug. 2. Expect weekday single-lane closures, reduced speed zones, and extra caution around Topanga blind curves.

Practical takeaway: check Caltrans QuickMap before using PCH or Topanga during construction hours, especially between Temescal Canyon Road, Sunset Boulevard, Carbon Beach Terrace, and the SR-27 intersection.

Returning residents hit a trash-bin problem

Circling The News reports that some residents with certificates of occupancy are being told they cannot receive trash bins until temporary LADWP construction power poles are removed. The Mayor’s recovery office said that should not block bins, but at least one resident reportedly got a different answer from sanitation staff.

Practical takeaway: if this affects you, the cited story says the Mayor’s office directed residents to LASAN’s 24/7 Customer Care Center at 1-800-773-2489.

Goldbrecht offers rebuild incentives for Palisades homeowners

Palisades News says Goldbrecht is offering an incentive program for Pacific Palisades homeowners rebuilding houses lost in the January 2025 fires. The article frames the offering around its glass wall systems for coastal homes.

Practical takeaway: this is a commercial rebuild option, not a public program; use it as one more vendor lead if you are comparing rebuild materials.

Station 69 engineer Yvonne Gutierrez is retiring after 33 years

Circling The News reports that LAFD Engineer Yvonne Gutierrez, celebrated at Station 69 on July 24, will retire August 1 after 33 years with LAFD. The story notes her years serving the Palisades community and her 2018 firefighter-of-the-year recognition from American Legion Post 283.

Practical takeaway: no action required, but it is a meaningful local public-safety milestone.
