Key Stories

Red Cross grant expands long-term fire recovery support

The Malibu Community Long Term Recovery Group received an American Red Cross grant to expand recovery work for Palisades, Malibu, and Topanga residents affected or displaced by the fire. The program says it has served more than 2,550 people, distributed $3.5 million in donated goods, and provided $3.3 million in cash assistance. Westside Current and Patch both summarize the announcement.

Why Rex should care: this is one of the more concrete recovery-support items today, especially for residents still dealing with case management, household recovery, or gaps in assistance.

Palisades Charter Elementary rebuild remains on the late-2026 construction track

Palisades Charter Elementary is expected to begin construction in the fourth quarter of 2026, after the fire destroyed about 70% of the campus. The project is estimated at roughly $134.9 million, with completion projected for the third quarter of 2028. Palisadian-Post has renderings and more detail.

The practical takeaway: the school rebuild is moving, but the return of students to a rebuilt campus is still a multi-year timeline.

Decisions Affecting Palisades

SB 1229 rebuild explainer says current fire survivors are not the target

A local real-estate explainer says SB 1229 would require Coastal Commission review when a new owner buys a fire-destroyed coastal property and wants to rebuild, but says the bill does not change the process for fire survivors rebuilding their own homes. Read the explainer.

Practical takeaway: if you are rebuilding your own lost home, this source says to keep moving; if you are selling or buying a burned lot, the buyer’s plan matters.

Potrero bridge viewpoint asks for a public-safety plan

A Circling The News viewpoint argues the proposed Potrero pedestrian bridge should come with dedicated police or public-safety presence at the beach end, near the Recreation Center, and near the Sunset/PCH crossing. Read the viewpoint.

Practical takeaway: this is opinion, not an official action, but it flags a resident concern to watch if bridge planning advances.
