Key Stories

PPCC election gives residents a civic calendar to track

Palisades News reports 17 candidates are running for Pacific Palisades Community Council seats. The practical dates: candidate forum Aug. 13, online voting Aug. 21 through Sept. 4.

The candidate statements center heavily on wildfire recovery, rebuilding, infrastructure, public safety, emergency preparedness, and preserving neighborhood character. If you only read one procedural story, make it this one.

PCH protective-wall work marks another fire-recovery milestone

The Malibu Times reports Caltrans completed two protective wall projects along PCH: one just south of Big Rock Drive and another near the Getty Villa. Together they represent nearly $27 million in repairs.

For residents, the takeaway is that PCH may feel open again, but the corridor remains a long-running fire-recovery construction and stabilization zone.

Will Rogers ecology and stargazing event points to everyday recovery

The Palisadian-Post covered a Los Angeles Astronomical Society and TreePeople event at Will Rogers State Historic Park, combining a postfire ecology walk with public stargazing.

This is not urgent, but it is useful: residents are again using the park as a place to learn, gather, and observe the landscape’s recovery.

Alphabet Streets pumpkin patch turns a burned lot into a hopeful local marker

Circling The News reports Bruce Schwartz is growing large pumpkins on an empty Alphabet Streets lot after the fire. The story is small but locally meaningful: a vacant burned-area lot is being used for visible renewal while rebuilding decisions continue around it.

Police chase ends in the ocean off Will Rogers State Beach

A KABC report carried by AOL says a felony-warrant suspect fled into the water at Will Rogers State Beach during a police chase. The report says lifeguards were near the suspect in the water; further details were not provided.
