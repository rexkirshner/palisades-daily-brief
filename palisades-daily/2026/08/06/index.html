Key Stories

Measure ULA exemption heads to November ballot

The Los Angeles City Council voted 13-1 to put a November ballot measure before voters that would create a one-time, five-year Measure ULA exemption for qualifying homes damaged or destroyed in the January 2025 Palisades Fire. The practical question is whether sellers of high-value damaged homes get transfer-tax relief through early 2030.

Read: Palisades News, NBC Los Angeles, LAist.

Palisades Village reopening becomes the main everyday comeback marker

Palisades Village is scheduled to reopen Aug. 15 after extensive restoration, with returning tenants and new restaurants. The useful resident takeaway is simple: the Village is becoming a real calendar date for normal commercial life, not just a future promise.

PAIGE is also returning when the center reopens. That specific retailer was recently covered, so treat today’s useful update as part of the broader Aug. 15 reopening picture.

Miramar homeowners make the resident case for private license-plate cameras

A Paseo Miramar homeowners letter supports tighter LAPD license-plate-reader contracts, audits, and documentation, but argues private cameras on resident property should not be swept into a ban. The letter frames the issue around single-access hillside safety, rebuild-related theft risk, and limited police presence.

Decisions Affecting Palisades

Farmers proposes more small-business coverage, with higher rates

Farmers filed a plan to write more than 1,500 new business-owner and business-property policies in wildfire-distressed California areas over two years, with an average proposed rate increase above 15%. For Palisades businesses, this is a background insurance-market signal rather than an immediate resident action.

Santa Monica shifts homelessness services under city control

Santa Monica approved a Housing Focused System of Care that gives the city more direct control over outreach, interim housing, and permanent-housing pathways. It is not a Palisades policy, but nearby homelessness-service changes can affect Westside service patterns and public-safety conversations.

Fire/Rebuild Watch

Federal recovery funding remains a political and fiscal pressure point

Governor Newsom called on President Trump to deliver delayed federal wildfire recovery funding for Altadena, Pacific Palisades, and Malibu. This is mostly background today unless federal funding movement becomes tied to a concrete homeowner program, school project, or local rebuilding allocation.

Rebuild-cost guides are useful, but compare scope carefully

Several contractor and architecture pages describe Palisades rebuild services and costs, including Estate Design & Construction, Ziese Architecture, DWD Builders, UBIC, and Westside Build. Use them as market background, not neutral cost benchmarks: they are provider materials, and figures may exclude different items.
