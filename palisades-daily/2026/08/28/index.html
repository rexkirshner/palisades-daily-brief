Key Stories

LADWP power delays are blocking reopenings and rebuild completions

Rodeo Realty owner Syd Leibovitch says LADWP delays have kept a finished Via de la Paz commercial building from reopening and delayed several residential projects. He is planning a lantern-lit community meeting at 6 p.m. Sept. 9 at 839 Via de la Paz.

Why it matters: this is no longer an abstract utility complaint; it is a move-back and local-employment bottleneck.

Marquez commercial district owners begin rebuild planning

Property owners in the Marquez Knolls commercial strip are beginning redevelopment planning, starting with demolition of remaining foundations. Owners hope to bring back a prefire-style business mix, with Vittorio’s, Knolls Pharmacy, TLC Beauty Salon and Ronny’s Park Lane Cleaners among businesses reported as interested.

Practical read: early stage, but important for residents who depend on the Marquez/Sunset neighborhood-serving corridor.

Pali High football returns to campus with Chargers support

Palisades High played its first home football game on campus since the fires on Aug. 21 at Stadium by the Sea, with Chargers players including Daiyan Henley attending. The Chargers photo gallery provides a visual record of the night.

Why it matters: this is school recovery becoming visible in ordinary community life again.

Returning residents have a direct number for trash-bin service

Circling The News reports that LA Sanitation says Palisades residents without permanent power can still request service. Residents are directed to call 800-773-2489 and use the fire option to speak with billing.

Practical takeaway: if you are back or near move-in and stuck without bins, this is the action item to try.

Decisions Affecting Palisades

County bulletin includes rebuild-inspection workshop and small-business finance help

The LA County Coordinated Joint Information Center bulletin includes a Sept. 24 virtual workshop on rebuilding inspections and a no-cost Financial Clinic program for small businesses. For Palisades residents, the inspections workshop is the more directly relevant rebuild item.

State wildfire-liability debate remains unresolved

The Orange County Register/Bloomberg report says California lawmakers are pushing back on a plan tied to utility wildfire liability and insurer recovery rights. Treat this as background unless it changes claim timing, utility exposure, or insurer behavior.

Mayoral race coverage is mostly political process

Fox News coverage includes voter criticism of the Bass-Raman debate and mentions Palisades fire response as part of broader LA dissatisfaction. It is relevant only as campaign context, not a new local action item.

Fire/Rebuild Watch

Fire-resident panel stresses evacuation over staying behind

At a La Señora Research Institute panel, five residents who stayed behind during the Palisades Fire described defending homes but urged ordinary residents to evacuate in future disasters. The most practical takeaway is preparedness, not imitation.

Burned-lot market lessons are useful for owners deciding whether to sell or rebuild

A Circling The News real-estate column says the predicted wave of distressed sales did not materialize and emphasizes pricing, buyer sophistication, buildability, and rebuild constraints. Useful if you are evaluating a lot, but not an official market report.

Second-residence checklist is a buyer due-diligence guide

A Pacific Palisades second-residence guide emphasizes verifying occupancy status, permits, utilities, insurance, governing documents, intended use, and absence planning for the exact address. It is practical background for anyone considering a Palisades purchase or rebuild-stage property.

Around The Web

Flour Pizzeria signs lease for a Palisades return

Flour Pizzeria plans to return to Pacific Palisades at the former Noah’s Bagels space at 15270 Antioch Street, targeting a 2027 opening. This is a small but concrete signal of everyday commercial recovery beyond Palisades Village.

FAIR Plan explainer is useful but not Palisades-specific

A Schneiderman Insurance explainer reviews how the California FAIR Plan evolved and notes that a homeowner carrying only a FAIR Plan policy has fire coverage and limited additional protection. Treat it as insurance background, not Palisades-specific advice.

IRS wildfire-relief FAQ remains relevant for claim and tax questions

The IRS wildfire-relief FAQ covers qualified wildfire relief payments and casualty losses. It may be worth saving if you are coordinating tax filings, amended returns, insurance proceeds, or disaster payments.
