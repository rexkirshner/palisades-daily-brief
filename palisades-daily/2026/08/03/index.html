Key Stories

Palisades leaders defend license-plate cameras as city weighs restrictions

Palisades representatives argued that automated license-plate cameras help compensate for limited policing during rebuild, especially around burglary and construction-site theft concerns. Opponents at the Police Commission forum raised surveillance, privacy, and data-use objections. Residents who support the cameras were told they have until Aug. 6 to comment. Palisadian-Post | Circling The News

Palisades General Store plans Highlands Plaza opening

Amy Russo, whose prior business space was destroyed in the January 2025 fire, plans to open Palisades General Store at 542 1/2 Palisades Drive. The store is expected to soft open in mid-August, with a Sept. 19 grand opening, adding a practical local-retail return in the Highlands. Palisadian-Post

Construction nails prompt street cleanup effort

Residents are reporting construction nails in Palisades streets during rebuild. Circling The News describes neighbors collecting nails and says an Alphabet-area cleanup with supplies is planned for Aug. 22 from 9 to 11 a.m.; more details are expected closer to the date. Circling The News

Bruce Lurie Gallery keeps Palisades ties while operating in Santa Monica

Bruce Lurie Gallery, formerly on Via de la Paz, is operating from Santa Monica after losing its Palisades space in the fire. Lurie says he wants to return to the Palisades when the right space is available, and one surviving sculpture from the former gallery will be displayed at Palisades Village. Palisadian-Post

Work truck crash into PCH-area building remains under investigation

A work truck crashed into a one-story commercial building at 14830 W. Pacific Coast Highway. Firefighters extricated the driver, no other injuries were reported, and city building, transportation, and police agencies were notified. Palisades News | FOX 11

Decisions Affecting Palisades

Senator Allen clarifies he is not joining Uplifters Foundation board

Sen. Ben Allen wrote that he supported Uplifters Foundation early, including a 501(c)(3) support letter, and had expressed willingness to serve, but no board agreement was finalized. Practical takeaway: residents evaluating Uplifters should treat Allen as supportive of recovery generally, not as part of the organization. Circling The News

Rebuild tensions with developers remain a Sacramento issue

The Real Deal reports continued conflict over postfire lot sales, investor activity, and state legislation affecting rebuilds. This matters as background for Palisades homeowners watching whether future rules distinguish between original-owner rebuilding and investor redevelopment. The Real Deal
