Key Stories

Coyotes are becoming a practical postfire safety issue

Circling the News reports increased coyote encounters, including an Aug. 2 report of a coyote under a dresser in a Via Floresta home and residents describing bolder behavior after the fire.

Practical takeaway: secure food waste, pet bowls, fences, walls, and pet doors; keep pets inside after dark; carry a deterrent such as a whistle or shaker can when walking.

Palisades Night becomes a monthly community gathering

Palisades News says Palisades Night will become a free first-Thursday monthly event at The Bungalow Santa Monica, starting Aug. 6 at 5 p.m. The next gathering features DJ Graham Funke, and merch sales benefit LA Strong Sports.

This is worth calendar attention if you want low-effort community connection during rebuild.

PAIGE plans Aug. 15 Palisades Village reopening

Daily News reports PAIGE will reopen its Palisades Village store Aug. 15, as the shopping center returns after more than $100 million in renovations and environmental remediation following the wildfire.

For residents, this is less about one retailer and more about the Village becoming usable again.

Decisions Affecting Palisades

Proposed wildfire-cost legislation remains one to watch

Los Angeles Times reports that fire survivors, consumer advocates, and insurance representatives are criticizing alleged last-minute state proposals they say could reduce utilities’ wildfire-cost exposure.

No bill text is cited in the article as released, so treat this as a watch item rather than an action item today. It matters because any eventual changes could affect recovery cost allocation for fire survivors and policyholders.

Fire/Rebuild Watch

Malibu rebuild delays remain useful comparison for Palisades

Spectrum News reports Malibu has issued 88 building permits, while more than 1,400 rebuilding permits have been issued for Palisades properties. Malibu officials cite terrain, shoreline erosion, bureaucracy, and coming El Niño-related rain and flooding risks.

For Rex: this is useful context for why neighboring coastal rebuilds may move at different speeds, especially along PCH.

Contractor marketing keeps targeting Palisades rebuilds

UBIC and Westside Build pages in today’s web results market Palisades fire-rebuild services. They may be useful as examples of how builders are pitching scope, permitting, insurance coordination, and rebuild management, but they are vendor marketing rather than civic news.
