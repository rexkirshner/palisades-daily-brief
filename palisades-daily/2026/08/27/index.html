Key Stories
  • PPCC voting is open through Sept. 4. Ballots are electronic through the council website; voters may select an area representative and an at-large representative. The candidate field includes longtime residents, displaced homeowners, property owners, and community volunteers focused on recovery.
  • Topanga Canyon Boulevard closes overnight Aug. 28-29. Caltrans says SR-27 will close between PCH and Grand View Drive from 11:59 p.m. Friday to 8 a.m. Saturday for installation of an 84-inch storm-drain pipe meant to reduce mud and debris-flow risk during storms.
  • Returning Palisadians are having trouble with trash pickup. The practical issue: some residents say they cannot get bins until power accounts transfer from builders to homeowners, while L.A. Sanitation says people should not have to wait.
