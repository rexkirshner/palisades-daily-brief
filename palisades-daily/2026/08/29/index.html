Key Stories
  • Palisades Optimist Club returns Sept. 1

The club’s first in-person and Zoom meeting is scheduled for 9:30 a.m. Tuesday, Sept. 1, at the Palisades Community Lutheran Church Fireside Room. The group is looking for new members and supports youth programs in and beyond the Palisades.

  • Residents urged to take follow-up NORC recovery survey

NORC at the University of Chicago, working with the Pacific Palisades Community Council and funded by the Riviera Foundation, is asking residents to participate in a follow-up survey on recovery and rebuilding priorities.

  • Palisades Rec Center playground replacement moves into view

The children’s playground is described as nearly 20 years old and not ADA compliant. The Park Advisory Board approved Shane’s Inspiration to guide a universally accessible playground process; the reported cost range is $300,000 to $700,000, depending on design.

Decisions Affecting Palisades
  • $250 million bond-backed home plan advances as rebuild debate background

A nonprofit plan would use up to $250 million in municipal bonds to build 60 homes priced around $3 million each in Pacific Palisades. The reporting says the City Council approved a second supporting resolution and that the deal requires no city or taxpayer-backed financial commitment.

  • Coverage from Briefs and Hoodline frames the same proposal as an experiment in accelerating reconstruction. Practical takeaway: this is not broad affordability relief, but it may influence the debate over vacant lots, tax base recovery and who gets back first.

Fire/Rebuild Watch
  • One family’s setback dispute shows permit friction is still personal

FOX 11 reports that the Pasternak family says its rebuild has been held up over a 5-foot setback issue. City officials said the family can appeal, and Councilmember Traci Park’s office said it is looking into the issue.

  • Calvary Church continues post-fire recovery and community use

Calvary’s sanctuary was destroyed, but the campus has resumed worship in the gym and is hosting community groups including Boy Scouts, AYSO leaders and Habitat for Humanity. The report says the sanctuary rebuild could take several years.

Around The Web
  • Recovery Labs emphasize deliberation over one-way public comment

A Palisadian-Post column describes Palisades Recovery Coalition labs as small-group deliberations where residents weigh tradeoffs on governance, wildfire resilience, utilities and rebuilding assistance.

  • Rustic Canyon pool is open through Labor Day

The pool at 601 Latimer Road is open weekdays 3 to 7 p.m. and weekends 1 to 5 p.m. until Labor Day. Admission is cash only, with lower rates for children, older adults and people with disabilities.

  • Palisades Village comeback coverage continues

City Lifestyle highlights returning and new Palisades Village businesses, including retailers, restaurants and neighborhood operators. Useful if you want a quick read on commercial recovery momentum, not a new official action.
