Key Stories

Palisades Village sets reopening-day details

Palisades Village will reopen Saturday, Aug. 15 with a 10:30 a.m. ribbon cutting, 11 a.m. store and restaurant openings, all-day activations, tastings, entertainment, family activities, and an 8 p.m. finale.

Practical takeaway: this is the clearest “what to expect” schedule for Saturday. Returning and new tenants include K Bakery, Elyse Walker, Porta Via, Angelini, Blue Ribbon Sushi, Alphabet Bar & Grill, and Spacca Tutto. Free parking runs through Sept. 30.

LADWP updates Palisades water infrastructure schedule

LADWP says three Palisades water projects are complete, two are under construction, and several more are set to begin within 90 days.

The active projects are North Amalfi/Romany, expected to finish by mid-September, and Monaco/San Remo, expected by the end of October. Next-up locations include Capri/Sunset, Sunset/Las Casas, Livorno/Marquez, and Castellammare/Tramonto.

Pali High back-to-school item doubles as local history

A Palisadian-Post school-history piece notes Pali High is preparing to begin a new year on campus while still using bungalows. The story is built around donated 1965 graduate mementos and the school’s old national-media profile.

Read it if you want a lighter schools item with local identity value rather than a policy update.

Pali High runner broke a long-standing City Section record

Circling The News reports Ernesto Jalomo broke a 42-year-old Los Angeles City Section 1600-meter record after transferring to Pali High, navigating eligibility limits, and later committing to run at UC Berkeley.

This is a school-sports feature, not a resident action item, but it is a real local achievement.

Measure ULA exemption remains a November ballot item

Westside Current recaps the proposed temporary Measure ULA transfer-tax exemption for qualifying Palisades properties damaged or destroyed in the 2025 wildfire.

No new resident deadline appears in today’s story beyond the November vote. Treat this as background unless you may sell a qualifying property or are tracking ballot language.

Fire/Rebuild Watch

Private rebuild-permit guide is useful only as vendor background

Ocean Permits describes rebuild-expediting services for Pacific Palisades, Altadena, and Malibu, including references to emergency orders, fee waivers, pre-approved plans, and agency differences.

Useful reminder: Pacific Palisades rebuilds run through City of Los Angeles agencies, not the same system as unincorporated Altadena. Because this is a private service page, treat it as background, not official guidance.

PRC frames Palisades recovery as a community model

The Palisades Recovery Coalition’s 2026 Community Report page describes PRC’s goal of building a resident-informed recovery organization that can also preserve lessons for other disaster-hit communities.

Read if you are following the civic architecture of recovery, not if you need a near-term permit, insurance, or construction action.
