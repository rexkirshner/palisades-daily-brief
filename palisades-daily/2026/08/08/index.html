Key Stories

Pali High photography teacher adds an adult SMC class

Circling The News reports that Rick Steil, who teaches beginning and AP photography at Palisades High, will teach an evening Santa Monica College photography class.

Practical takeaway: adults who wanted access to his instruction now have a college-credit option. The class is listed for Thursdays from 6:45 to 9:50 p.m.; SMC classes start August 30, and the first photography class is September 3.

Decisions Affecting Palisades

Santa Monica takes direct control of homelessness services

Palisades News reports that Santa Monica approved a Housing Focused System of Care, shifting more control over homelessness services from the county-run system to the city.

Why Rex should care: it affects nearby outreach, interim housing, motel use, security expectations, and the closure/replacement path for SaMoShel. It is not a Palisades decision, but it could affect the broader coastal Westside service and public-safety environment.

Insurance commissioner race becomes a homeowner issue

The Los Angeles Times reports that State Sen. Ben Allen and Jane Kim have split major endorsements in the race for California insurance commissioner.

Practical takeaway: this is a political-process story for now, but the office matters directly to Palisades residents because the article frames the race around insurer exits, premiums, and wildfire-claim fallout.

Fire/Rebuild Watch

Measure ULA fire exemption remains November-ballot background

The Real Deal reports on the Los Angeles City Council’s 13-1 vote to put a Palisades Fire-related Measure ULA exemption before voters.

This has been covered recently, so the useful update is just confirmation from another outlet: the proposal is a one-time, five-year transfer-tax exemption for qualifying sales of fire-damaged or destroyed homes, with debate over limiting relief to primary-residence homeowners.
