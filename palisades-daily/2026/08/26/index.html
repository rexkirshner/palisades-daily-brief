Key Stories

Via Bluffs residents launch private security program

The Via Bluffs Conservancy has hired ACS Security for at least one manned patrol car 16 hours a day and is working toward four mobile surveillance units at neighborhood entry and exit points. Organizers say the effort responds to vacant properties, limited police presence, and theft risks as construction materials return. Palisadian-Post

The program is voluntary, not an HOA. The article reports organizers are seeking at least $36,000 per month, with proposed contributions of $250 per month for standing or under-construction homes and $100 per month for vacant lots.

Infrastructure work prepares Palisades for wet weather

Bienveneda’s fire-damaged bulkhead is largely complete, Enchanted Way work is finished, and additional Castellammare-area bulkheads are in design and bidding. In the Highlands, debris basin No. 69 is being cleared, with 20 to 25 trucks hauling material daily and occasional one-lane conditions on Palisades Drive. Palisades News

PCH work near Porto Marina Way includes wall restoration, a lane reduction, and a 35 mph speed limit expected to continue through at least the end of the year. Patch

Pali High adds girls flag football

Palisades High’s new girls flag football team held its first practice at Stadium by the Sea, with 26 players on the roster after about 70 girls tried out. The inaugural season begins September 10 at Los Angeles Center for Enriched Studies; the first home game is September 14 against University at 3 p.m. Palisadian-Post

Pali High girls golf starts September 1

The Dolphins open September 1 against Marymount at Rancho Park at 3:30 p.m. The team is trying to win a sixth straight City title, which would tie the section team-championship record. Palisadian-Post

Decisions Affecting Palisades

State wildfire-liability negotiations remain unsettled

CalMatters reports lawmakers have opposed several parts of Gov. Gavin Newsom’s wildfire-liability proposal, including limits affecting survivor compensation, insurer reimbursements, and local-government recovery for destroyed infrastructure. The article says the legislative session ends August 31 and no proposal text had been released in the reporting. CalMatters

For Palisades residents, this is worth watching as future-fire policy and utility accountability background, not as a direct action item today.

City Planning schedules Missing Middle outreach in Westchester

Los Angeles City Planning will hold an outreach event August 29 on Missing Middle LA, focused on housing types such as duplexes, small-lot homes, and ADUs. The source says the initiative aims at neighborhood-scale housing options across the city, including Venice. Palisades News

Fire/Rebuild Watch

LADWP and city mediation is a legal-process development for fire victims

Circling the News reports attorneys for fire victims reached an agreement with LADWP and the City of Los Angeles to mediate, with the first session most likely in September. The source says other defendants may join, but as of the article it was just DWP and the city. Circling the News

This matters for fire-victim litigation, but today’s source does not report a resident filing deadline or settlement terms.

Rebuild documentation services are appearing in the recovery market

A private drone-documentation provider is marketing dated aerial, ground, video, and 360-degree milestone records for wildfire rebuilds in Malibu, Pacific Palisades, and Los Angeles. Treat this as a services-market signal for owners and builders, not a civic update. Drones Point of View

Palisades Community Church pastors held a first Beach Spirit Walk along Will Rogers Beach on August 22. Circling the News reports participants spoke about returning, rebuilding, and gratitude, and that organizers may try monthly or every-other-month walks because of interest. Circling the News
